# Community conduct

Treat contributors and creators with respect. Welcome beginners, give concrete technical feedback, credit research and artwork, and distinguish disagreement from personal criticism. Harassment, threats, doxxing, discriminatory abuse, plagiarism, credential theft and distributing others' private or proprietary files are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation proportionately. Report concerns through GitHub's private reporting options where available, or contact a repository maintainer through their publicly listed channel. Do not post a victim's private details in a public issue. No unverified private contact address is asserted here.

This policy applies to this project's repository and project-operated community spaces. It does not claim to govern the independent Halo communities we credit.
