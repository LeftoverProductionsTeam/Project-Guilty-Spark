# Help and problem reports

Use [GitHub issues](https://github.com/LeftoverProductionsTeam/Project-Guilty-Spark/issues/new/choose) for reproducible app problems, edition compatibility, rendering, geometry, tool setup, research and attribution corrections. Choose the matching beginner-friendly template. Include the app version, Halo edition/build, exact action, expected result and a small safe diagnostic report.

Before attaching files, remove personal paths, account identifiers and secrets. Do not attach game maps, extracted images/audio, tags, editing-kit archives, executables or paid content. A source SHA-256 and a path-free material diagnostic can identify a problem without uploading the source. Follow [Getting Started](docs/GETTING_STARTED.md) for the game-versus-editing-kit distinction.

Community research links are credited references, not promises that independent tool authors provide support for Spark. Multiplayer, restored Zombie Apocalypse builds, cross-engine portability, Mac/PowerPC/Linux, VR and split-screen still have separate acceptance gates.
