# Assets, copyright and source preservation

The project's MIT license covers its original code; it does not grant rights to Halo, Microsoft/Bungie/Gearbox/Saber content, mod creators' assets, official tools or third-party software.

Use legally obtained local game files. Preserve source hashes and original authorship. Projects store source-bound local references and edits. Bitmap previews are transient local decoding, not permission to redistribute extracted textures. Do not commit maps, tags, source art/audio, tool/game binaries, proprietary SDK material, decompiled implementation, account credentials, installation details or private logs.

Construct parser and archive tests from synthetic bytes. Public diagnostics should contain counts, supported/unsupported reasons and hashes; review screenshots for personal paths and confidential details. A rendered application screenshot documents its visible behavior and does not license the underlying game art for unrelated reuse.

Do not remove authorship or circumvent protection to make a recovery appear successful. Keep unsupported source mechanisms and restoration defects visible. Rebuilding or porting a mod requires permission for its constituent content and a valid target-specific workflow.

Report attribution corrections through the supplied issue template. Maintainers review incoming assets and licenses before accepting or publishing them. Separate installer/system prerequisites and optional external tools retain their own terms; see [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
