# To everyone who kept Halo modding alive

> Thank you to the people who reversed tag structures, preserved map files, wrote a forum guide, patched a forgotten tool, built a multiplayer arena, tested against obscure editions, organized archives, or answered a beginner's question. Guilty Spark grows from those shared discoveries.

## Attribution principles

- This is **not** an exhaustive registry of every contributor. Some historical forums and source links have disappeared, and handles can be ambiguous; do not invent individuals or assign ownership from filenames alone.
- Include a link to the strongest surviving primary source when available, plus a contribution description. The user's editable nominations are **unverified** and local-only until someone checks them.
- Keep the difference clear between community history, executable rights, source-code licenses and rights to distribute Halo copyrighted assets.
- No binaries, decompiled proprietary game code, copyrighted textures or audio are bundled with this project.

## An editor built for teaching

The safest tutorial is one that explains **what the tool really writes**, and **how to reverse it**. For every feature prefer:

1. A real, demonstrable task, with the exact source format/engine version stated.
2. A friendly tooltip describing the action, its dependency and its side effects.
3. A clean source snapshot/backup and a rollback path before any tool writes.
4. One visible verification result, not a fake green success badge.
5. Credits and references to the people who published the techniques.

Different Halo runtimes use different data layouts and toolchains. The integrated console runs only approved commands and prints their output; a separate Windows editor cannot magically become a native embedded editor, and a live editor preview is not a running Halo game.

See the Community Thanks page in-app to suggest and export additional sourced nominations.
