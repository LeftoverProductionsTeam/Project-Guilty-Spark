## The product

Project Guilty Spark / Halo Modern Evolved is a **single unified creator application**, **not** a launcher for separate editors. The vision combines:

- Eschaton/HMT-like typed/tagged editing and cross-file references; SwordEdit/Sapien-like 3D maps, objects, terrain, caves, spawns, game-type flags and editable scene placement; real original BSP data rather than proxy boxes.
- Visual material discovery: automatic bitmap/shader extraction from *owned* maps and resources; real sky, fog, lighting, weather, animated materials and original audio with controls in the editor; optional Halo classic textures/sounds sourced from the user's own game files.
- Creation, importing and exporting: OBJ/JMS/related assets, shaders, texture UVs, tagged objects and assets; rebuilt, truly valid BSP/collision/portals/PVS; stable known toolchains hidden behind one coherent workflow.
- One-click-ish but honest **compile, package, launch and test** gates with target-specific receipts, meaningful failures and no bogus “playable” status.
- A GUI for **Slayer, CTF, Oddball, King of the Hill hills, Race**, user-created modes and source maps; multiplayer/session/server controls; optional Firefight/zombie-survival waves, AI, weapons, item triggers and campaign mission/cinematics, as supported by the target engine.
- Major flagship proof projects: **Guilty Spark Test Arena**, original **Zombie Apocalypse** recovered from owned Halo Demo sources while preserving historical behavior/credits, and **First Contact** playable campaign vertical slice.
- Halo Demo/PC/Custom Edition, HaloMD, MCC H1A and separately original Mac G4 workflows. Capabilities vary by runtime. Online AI behavior, HaloScript extensions and server hosting require engine-specific verification, not wishful toggles.
- Safe original-source preservation, owner-controlled tool execution, auditable diagnostics, meaningful undo and a context-rich **Modding Academy** with creator credit and historical tool documentation.
