# Living Halo — mandatory unresolved engineering gates

These are NOT implemented by the continuation source patch. They are required to satisfy the full Project Guilty Spark vision and must remain tracked:

- **Halo CE material rendering:** model/BSP shader-to-bitmap dependencies; user-supplied `bitmaps.map` and source cache; resource-map versions; decoding DXT and other formats, original UVs; transparencies and detail maps; original lightmaps, sky and atmosphere. The renderer presently produces genuine geometry with a single-color diagnostic material.
- **All scenery models:** single-root GBX preview now structurally reads on many Halo Demo assets. Five of 35 scenery types require multi-node/skeletal transform support on the tested map; other Halo variants untested. No shader texture binding yet.
- **BSP editing:** compiled BSP is not an editable original source project. Provide a reversible reconstruction/import path, brush or mesh modeling controls, robust surface edits, topological validation, collision and portal regeneration. Introduce hill sculpting, texture painting, heightfields, rock faces, connected caves/tunnels and branching chamber authoring as real source operations with compile gates. Prevent collision holes: closed solid meshes, correct portals, appropriate visibility generation and navigation/collision/lightmap rebuilds.
- **Render distance:** editor camera distance is separate from original Halo's engine clipping/PVS/fog and runtime-specific extended draw-distance mods. Offer per-runtime proven capabilities, safe fallbacks and performance measurements, not one universal magic slider.
- **General Tool Manager:** prior Living Render patch includes only opt-in, validated cached Invader command-line utilities. Complete a capability/version registry, user-approved download/update, offline copies with SHA-256, license/provenance and rollback. Never cache/run decompiled proprietary or untrusted executable material automatically.
- **Playable builds:** official MCC Halo CE Editing Kit was not detected in historical Codex logs. JMS export and static geometry inspection are not valid proof of a compiled, playable BSP. Validate real map creation, collision/lighting and in-game loading separately.
- **Classic tag field breadth:** existing typed inspector covers a subset of tags; complete sound/shader/model/physics/AI field schemas, editable reflexives and safe dependency changes using version-specific structures and change history.
- **Original high-poly assets:** OBJ UV, normal, group preservation is improved; still need Blender/meshoptimizer integration, LOD, full material texture import, appropriate Halo render and collision tag compilation, animation support and meaningful visual/collision validation.
- **Campaign/cinematic/Firefight:** real mission authoring, HaloScript output and compilation, cinematics, dialogs/checkpoints/AI firing/navigation, playable First Contact and Zombie Apocalypse Firefight remain substantive future engineering.
- **Zombie Apocalypse:** the original damaged sound data and unusual vehicle-based actor spawning cannot be silently removed. Faithful MCC conversion remains blocked until actual valid source behavior and engine compatibility are established.
- **Multiplayer/collaboration:** versioned mod packaging and second-machine installation; compatibility checks; supported hosts/lobbies; synchronized networked AI on two real clients; privacy and rollback; team revision conflict resolution and Playtest Lab.
- **Authentic visual themes and audio:** use local player-provided `ui.map`/`bitmaps.map`/`sounds.map` only where lawful; build complete Theme Forge WYSIWYG bitmap layouts, UI sound choice, four presets and original fallback. No generated Halo art and no copyrighted assets in installers.
- **PowerPC Halo Mac Demo:** version-specific read/edit/build/play; original-G4 hardware acceptance; optional lightweight local-network Legacy Bridge after a valid export exists.
- **Mota-Lev interoperability:** research provenance and licensing of supplied archive; clean independent adapters and map/BSP swap/merge interoperability; do not execute untrusted archived programs or reuse proprietary decompiled code.
- **Cross-platform release and QA:** Windows full build and install, macOS/Linux where feasible, content-independent tests, evidence status, accessibility, performance, error recovery, licensing/privacy and explicit manual-acceptance inventory.

Full original Phase 3–7 prompts were not all recoverable verbatim from the available exported Codex log. Do not certify any requirements matrix as a *lossless text merger* until all full original source prompts have been obtained and compared line by line.
