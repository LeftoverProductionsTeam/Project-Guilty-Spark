# 2. FINISH MEGAWORLD — MAKE THE WORLD PHYSICALLY PLAYABLE

The existing Timberland–Sidewinder prototype has passed a scoped native CE compilation, independent readback, 524 compiled collision samples and Chupathingy map loading.

**Close those gaps first, then generalize the solution to the entire 19-map world.**

## A. Finish the first connection

- Open two genuine Halo maps.
- Select the source BSPs.
- Select specific edges or terrain surfaces.
- Choose the intended connection points.
- Generate an initially simple, low-poly terrain bridge.

- Create roads, caves, tunnels and mountain paths.

- Cut genuine openings and arches into supported geometry.
- Preview resulting collision, materials and portal regions.

- Launch the exact compiled content in Halo.

**Vehicle route:** A genuine road, open canyon or tunnel capable of accommodating a Warthog, with proper turning clearance, approach grades, landing surfaces and collision.

**High-ground route:** Connected upper terrain, cliffs and mountain ridges suitable for traversal, sniper sightlines and alternate tactical movement.

The lower road must not satisfy high-ground checks by accident.

- Portal placement, visibility and PVS diagnostics.
- Cave, tunnel and cliff entrances.
- Road slope and turn-radius diagnostics.
- Native material, UV, shader and lightmap continuity.
- Reusable construction operations with meaningful Undo/Redo.
- Real preview updates without unnecessary full reinspection.
- Clear, localized compiler-error visualization.

Prefer proven existing native and computational geometry facilities. Avoid another duplicate brush/mesh engine.

Do not silently discard native scenery, detail objects, source resources or user edits to make a build pass.
