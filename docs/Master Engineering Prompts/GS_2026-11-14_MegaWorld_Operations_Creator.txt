# GUILTY SPARK — COMPLETE MEGAWORLD OPERATIONS CREATOR

**ALL THE TOOLS, AND THEN SOME.**

Build a complete visual mission-authoring workflow for Halo-inspired cooperative and PvPvE Extraction. Preserve the entire existing roadmap.

### Reuse existing implementations

Extend Creator Studio, Mission Director, QuickForge, Logic / Events, native scenario editing, HaloScript export, Firefight/AI tools, Sound Mixer, asset library, viewport, Save, Undo/Redo and native compilation.

Do not create a competing mission editor, script engine, asset system, or build pipeline.

### Mission creation

Creators must be able to design complete replayable operations inspired by the military missions in Halo's novels.

Support reusable objective families:

- Reconnaissance and intelligence gathering
- Marine and civilian rescue, defense and escort
- Covenant elimination, facility assault and base capture
- Flood infestation discovery and containment
- Forerunner artifact and technology recovery
- Vehicle, supply and equipment salvage
- Infiltration, sabotage and demolition
- Communications restoration and defense
- High-value-target elimination
- Extraction, evacuation, failure and redeployment

Build on the existing mission beats and visual Logic / Events system.

Creators must be able to define objective dependencies, optional branches, alternate outcomes, failure conditions, rewards and mission escalation.

An operation can change objectives as events unfold. No need to hardcode individual quests.

### Complete visual authoring

Enable creators to place, select, move and configure:

- Spartan deployments and squad-specific insertion points
- Pelican landing and extraction areas
- Marine survivors, enemies, vehicles and equipment
- AI squads, patrols, defenses, reinforcements and difficulty
- Trigger volumes, terminals, switches and interactable objects
- Salvage, collectible intelligence and recoverable equipment
- Rescue routes, objectives and enemy-controlled locations
- Medical and revive equipment locations
- Extraction zones and multiplayer encounter areas

Use actual viewport controls and existing native writers. Everything must support appropriate source review, Save, Undo/Redo, restart and native readback.

### Voice, audio and presentation

Expand the existing Sound Mixer and cinematic/mission tools to author:

- UNSC radio transmissions
- Marine distress calls and dialogue
- ONI mission briefings
- AI warnings, squad notifications and objective updates
- Enemy transmissions and environmental sounds
- Combat music transitions, alarms and extraction countdowns
- Subtitles and synchronized dialogue/camera events

Support creator-provided recordings and existing authorized Halo audio resources.

Cues need editable triggers, playback scope, priority, timing, position, attenuation and preview.

Do not duplicate the existing audio engine or bundle proprietary game assets.

### Multiplayer operation authoring

Creators configure:

- Squads of up to six players
- Separate missions and progress for different squads
- Cooperative, PvE and PvPvE rules
- Optional temporary alliances and rival squad encounters
- Downed/revive rules and medical resources
- Loot ownership, death drops and extraction rewards
- Persistent weapon, armor and inventory progression
- Regional spawning and quests across MegaWorld

128 total players and 19 connected regions are eventual acceptance targets, not existing proven capabilities.

### Spark–Forerunner boundary

Reuse the existing Spark integration contract.

Native Halo maps, tags, AI encounters, triggers, HUD messages, sounds and HaloScript remain native wherever compatible.

For Forerunner-only features requiring independent squad state, define the smallest versioned supplemental mission data necessary. Do not add proprietary extensions to original Halo tags.

Export must validate required assets, identifiers, references and native readback. Unsupported target runtimes must report limitations clearly.

### First complete operation

Build an original mission called **Operation Black Echo**:

1. Deploy a squad into hostile territory.
2. Locate and rescue stranded Marines.
3. Investigate a Covenant facility.
4. Recover classified technology.
5. Encounter an optional Flood containment emergency.
6. Defend an extraction point.
7. Extract survivors and recovered equipment.

Include creator-configurable enemy encounters, branching objectives, radio dialogue, sound effects, subtitles, HUD updates, rewards and replayability.

### Acceptance

Verify the complete creator workflow:

Create → place assets and encounters → author quests/audio → Save → restart → reopen → native compile → independent readback → launch in Forerunner → observe mission execution → revise.

Use proportionate focused tests throughout; keep full-scale gameplay acceptance as a separate milestone.

Protect all original files and installations. Keep Forerunner read-only. Never push without approval.

**Finish functional creator workflows, not isolated controls. Continue the remaining roadmap autonomously.**
