Distinguish three camera capabilities
Editor camera: Spark controls its own viewport and records camera paths.

Native cinematic camera: Spark generates valid supported Halo camera/script source that can be compiled into the selected runtime.

Live in-game camera: Available only through verified runtime-specific interfaces.

Do not claim that editor camera movement automatically controls a live MCC/CE/OpenCE client.

Never rely on anti-cheat bypasses or unsupported memory hooks.
