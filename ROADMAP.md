# Roadmap

The [living requirements ledger](docs/MASTER_FEATURE_LEDGER.md) retains the original 92-item reconstruction, every supplied historical crosswalk row and later founder requests. This overview does not replace it.

## Next verification gates

1. Compare the actual textured Demo scene against the same original mod in its real game. Extend supported detail/blending/sky paths with version-specific fixtures.
2. Connect a legal complete H1A/CE kit and base tagset. Compile the smallest original arena in an isolated job, then test collision, Slayer and the five-mode fixture in the correct engine.
3. Verify two-client play, hosting and engine-specific AI synchronization independently. A network design or source simulator is not multiplayer acceptance.
4. Restore Zombie Apocalypse's original dependency/weapon/vehicle mechanisms without silently replacing them; preserve First Contact campaign and cinematic goals.

## Continued work

Real tag and dependency editing/transfer; BSP reconstruction and sculpting; room/cave/Boolean joins; collision/portals/PVS/radiosity; OBJ/JMS and supported interchange; all game modes and Firefight; scripting/campaign/cinematics; packaging/community discovery/Academy; faithful local Halo interface assets and sounds; compiler/runtime integration; backup/recovery/accessibility.

Co-op, expanded player counts, split-screen, VR, live engine editing, Mac Demo/PowerPC exports, native Apple Silicon/Linux and legacy companion validation remain long-term goals with explicit research and runtime gates. None are removed or advertised as completed by C33.
